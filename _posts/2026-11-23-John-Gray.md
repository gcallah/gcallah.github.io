# John Gray

John Gray is someone who has interested me for many, many years. I had the
opportunity to attend his lectures while I was at the LSE, but, stupidly, I
failed to take advantage of it. But then I began regularly reviewing his books
because, while I often disagreed with him, he always intrigued me. In any case,
I have made up for missing his lectures by being in touch with him the last
couple of years, including a lengthy interview.

If you're interested in learning a bit about him, a good place to start would
be that interview with him in *Modern Age*:

[![John Gray: In Conversation](https://modernagejournal.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/1-copy-3-1.png){: style="width: 67%; display: block; margin: 0 auto"}](https://modernagejournal.com/john-gray-in-conversation/237996/)

If you get through that, then you can look at the John Gray festschrift that I
edited:

[*Cosmos + Taxis*, John Gray Festschrift](https://cosmosandtaxis.org/ct-141112/)
