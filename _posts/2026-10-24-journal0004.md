October 24th 


We had a gig in Sumter, scheduled to start a little after 6. Gordon, Eamon, and I all planned on showing up around 4 to set up the PA, the drum kit, etc. I told Mike to make sure he got there by 5. By 5:15, we started calling him. Eamon finally got a hold of his pastor, who was supposed to drop him off. His pastor said Mike had never even asked him to be dropped off in Sumter. When my uncle finally got a hold of him, Mike told him that "I overslept." 

He overslept, but what did he oversleep? There was apparently no one he asked for a ride to the gig. 

The party was very nice. It was a "white party." There was almost no dance floor, and therefore, almost no one danced, but the people all seemed to enjoy it. 

The birthday lady had a "wardrobe malfunction" that delayed her appearance by a bit. She finally came out in a very glittery, low-cut dress. She sang the first three songs with us and did a great job. Daniel wowed them all with his rendition of "Unchained Melody." 

We took a significant break for dinner. I didn't have anything, because I cannot sing and eat: the pressure on my diaphragm from singing winds up making me burp into the microphone. And nobody wants to hear that. 

We actually didn't get done playing until around 9:30, a couple of hours later than I expected. Another 40 minutes to pack up, get some photos taken, and say goodbye, and I wasn't on the road back to Columbia until after 10.

And Eamon has a church gig at 8 in the morning!
