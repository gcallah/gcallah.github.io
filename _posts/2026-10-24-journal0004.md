October 24th 

I woke up and Elen gave me a haircut on the back porch.

She left with her parents for Myrtle Beach. She wanted to help them get down to
their timeshare. They're approaching 90, and a 2 hour drive on the highway
by either of them is starting to seem a little risky to us.

We have a gig in Sumter, scheduled to start a little after 6. Gordon, Eamon, and
I all planned on showing up around 4 to set up the PA, the drum kit, etc. I told
Mike to make sure he got there by 5. By 5:15, we started calling him. Eamon
finally got a hold of his pastor, who was supposed to drop him off. His pastor
said Mike had never even asked him to be dropped off in Sumter. When my uncle
finally got a hold of him, Mike told him that "I overslept." 

He overslept, but what did he oversleep? There was apparently no one he asked
for a ride to the gig. 

The party was very nice. It was a "white party." There was almost no dance
floor, and therefore, almost no one danced, but the people all seemed to enjoy
it. 

I think I mentioned that ever since I discovered that the whole "white party"
idea had been started by Sean Combs, I have been worried about being sex
trafficked if I attend this white party. Trying to adopt a "I've been given
lemons, let me make lemonade approach," I told my bandmate "Well, if I am sex
traffic, at least I'll be having a lot more sex. I mean, true, it's quite likely
to be rough and unpleasant sex, but there *will* be a lot more of it."

The birthday lady had a "wardrobe malfunction" that delayed her appearance by a
bit. She finally came out in a very glittery, low-cut dress. She sang the first
three songs with us and did a great job. Daniel wowed them all with his
rendition of "Unchained Melody." 

We took a significant break for dinner. I didn't have anything, because I cannot
sing and eat: the pressure on my diaphragm from singing winds up making me burp
into the microphone. And nobody wants to hear that. 

We actually didn't get done playing until around 9:30, a couple of hours later
than I expected. Another 40 minutes to pack up, get some photos taken, and say
goodbye, and I wasn't on the road back to Columbia until after 10.

And Eamon has a church gig at 8 in the morning!

===============

To end a long day, when I got home, I found that my car had been broken into.
The thief took my backpack, which contained my laptop, my camera, and my wallet.
I had to cancel all of my credit cards and debit cards, and I have to get a new
driver's license.

===============

In any case, to end a long day, when I was feeding one of my cats some liquidy
meat stuff out of a tube, the liquidy meat stuff shot up into my eye when I tore
open the tube.

Another item of note: that cat, the skittish one, often will call one of us out
into the kitchen. We arrive expecting that she needs something. Well, in one
sense, I guess she does: she feels she needs us to watch her eat. There was food
in her bowl already and she had been eating it but when we left the room, she
came to wherever we went and called us back so we could finish watching or eat.

When given a gift of a golden crown encrusted with a multitude of precious gems,
it's a very bizarre response to complain about the one jewel that is missing in
one of the crowns orifices

And sometimes, when God sends a special gift to us, I think rather than letting
it go to our heads, we should more likely think "God thought I was such a
knucklehead that I needed this special gift to wake me up."
