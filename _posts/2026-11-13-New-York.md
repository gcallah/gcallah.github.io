# If You Are Tired of New York...

Samuel Johnson famously said, "No, Sir, when a man is tired of London, he is
tired of life; for there is in London all that life can afford."

I think the same thing is true of New York City. I went out for a walk last
night after ten. I was talking to my closest friend on the phone, when I saw
three guys seemingly pushing a table on wheels up the street. They looked like
they were struggling mightily with it , but I could not figure out why, since
it was on wheels. 

But then it came off the wheels, and I realized I was completely mistaken: They
were using a *walker* as a sort of very bad hand truck to transport this very
large tabletop. They went a couple buildings further down the street, and then
came back my way Sons table top. At that point, I realized they were not just
using any Walker : They were using the Walker that one of them needed to get
around. And that explained why two guys seemed to be actually moving the table
top, while the third seemed to be more using it for support.

A couple of minutes later, the three came back my way again... with a second
large tabletop balanced precariously on the walker. I am really struggling to
come up with a plausible explanation for what this operation was that I was
witnessing.
