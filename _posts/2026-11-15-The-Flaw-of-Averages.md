# The Flaw of Averages 

At dinner Friday night, I was introducing two of my children to a former
swimming teammate of mine. 

"Kids, your father has been a swimmer for years. But did you know that my friend
and I, between us, have earned All-American honors 19 times?"

"Now, it was, in fact, 19 times for her, and 0 for me, but still, it is true
that between the two of us, we averaged 9.5 All-American awards."

My friend Nick was immediately intrigued by this idea. When I mentioned Joe
DiMaggio, he immediately piped up, "Do you know the career home run average of
me and Joe DiMaggio? Over 200!"

I added, "And do you know what the average net worth of me and Elon Musk is?
Over $500 billion."

I think this idea is almost as fruitful as being able to tell people about all
the natural disasters you have survived, e.g.: 

"Did you know that I survived the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami?"

"Wow, how did you make it through that?"

"Well, chiefly by being nowhere near the Indian Ocean at that time. It's one of
the best ways to survive natural disasters: don't be anywhere near where they
happen."
