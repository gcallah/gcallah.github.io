# Santo: The Demented Dwarf 

![Santo](/graphics/DementedDwarf.jpg)

Our title today comes from Gerry Scott: when I would mention to Gerry that I had
seen Santo, he would usually ask "How is the demented dwarf?"

Gerry had met Santo when Santo and two other fellows accosted him, threatening
violence. Gerry knocked Santo out with a single punch, and the other two fellows
ran away. But now Gerry was faced with an unconscious man on the ground below
him, so he called a car, and when it came, hauled Santo into it, and asked the
driver to take them to the hospital. Santo came to during the car ride and asked
where he was. When Gerry explained the situation to him, Santo asked, "So you
knocked me out, and now you're bringing me to the hospital?" From that point on,
Santo regarded Gerry as a true friend. I think if Gerry had only done one of
those things... knocked him out, *or* brought him to the hospital... Santo would
not have been so impressed. It was one person doing both that got him hooked. 

At that time, Santo was trim and fit. But by the time I met him, he weighed
almost 300 lbs., most of it in his enormous belly. And note: he was only 5'5"
tall. As opposed to most men with a belly that large, Santo's didn't droop down,
and it looked hard: more like he was pregnant than like he was fat.

He also had the largest naval hernia I have ever seen. It looked like someone
had copied his belly again, just at a smaller scale. One day Gerry got a phone
call from Santo saying, "My hernia has turned green. What should I do?"

Gerry told him, "Well Santo, if some part of my body is turning green, generally
I would go to the hospital."

Was Santo my friend? Kind of: it was sort of like being friends with a wild
honey badger. 

One day, he asked me, "Professor, when is your new book coming out?" (I wasn't
actually a professor yet, but from the day he met me, that's what he called me.)

I told him, "Next month." 

He asked, "Can I get an autographed copy?" 

"Of course."

He got a big smile on his face. "Good, that way I can off you, and drive the
price way up. Nothing makes an author famous like being murdered."

After that, I would regularly complain to other patrons at the bar, "This guy
here claims he's my friend, and he promised to kill me to make me more famous.
But it turns out it was all just empty words."

Santo also had the lovely habit of occasionally, in the middle of talking,
simply grabbing your balls and squeezing real hard. After a couple times, I told
him he better cut it out. He took offense at this, gave me a glare, and said,
"What are you going to do about it?" 

I told him I was going to stop him physically, and unless he was prepared to
kill me, he'd have to accept that. He stopped.

A few years later, Santo had a stroke. After his stroke, Santo took it easy. And
by "took it easy," I mean he sat around his house with a couple of young
followers, smoking crack and drinking. 

Surprisingly, given this health regimen, he died about a year later from a heart
attack.

RIP you demented dwarf.
