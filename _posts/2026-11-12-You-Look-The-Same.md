# You Look the Same

At an event in my hometown last night I saw several people who I had not seen
since high school. At least three different people commented to me "You look
exactly the same."

Now I know they're trying to compliment me. And I don't think I look bad for
sixety-seven. But I also thought, "Criminy, if I looked like this when I was 16, people
back then must have thought "There is a 16 year old with something terribly
wrong with him!'"
