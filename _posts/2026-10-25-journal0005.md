# October 25, 2026

It was a quiet day today. Eamon had to go play the church at 8 AM, so he let the
chickens out early, then I went down and fed them a little while later.

I loafed around for a little while, then unloaded my car, which was entirely
stuffed front-to-back with music equipment, into my uncle's house next door.

Then I drove down to Whole Foods and picked up lamb shanks to make for dinner
for me and Eamon. I'm going to make lamb with green chilies, mint, and methi
leaves. 

I sautéed onions, added ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, and cumin seeds. I
browned the lamb shanks in a frying pan, then put them in with the sauté. I took
the Mint leaves, half a dozen green chilies, and some yogurt and water, and
blended them together, and poured them over the lamb. Then I got two cubes of
frozen methi leaves from the freezer and put them in the pot, waited till they
broke up, then threw the pot in the oven for a few hours. 

It being Sunday, and me being a good American, of course I watched some
football. Then I left "for the gym." But I wound up having such a great
conversation on the way over that I actually sat in the parking lot
talking until it was time to go to mass. Whoa, what a workout!

When I got home, the food was ready, but first I had to put the chickens to bed.

I plan on reading *The Crucible*  a little more before bedtime, but I am already
feeling sleepy.
